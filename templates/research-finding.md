# Research finding record

Copy into the relevant evidence package and replace prompts with specific content.
An empty form is `template_only`, not a finding. Follow the
[research-to-model workflow](../docs/research/research-to-model-workflow.md).

## Identity, question, and scope

- Finding ID/revision, researcher/reviewer, and dates:
- One falsifiable physical question and decision it controls:
- Phase, original `Pxx-Tyy` task, exact `ENG-Pxx-Tyy` work order, and requirements:
- Source commit, current model/configuration revision:
- Specimen/configuration/session/player scope, or `synthetic`:
- Observable, unit, conditions, and predeclared acceptance rule:

Name the current assumption that may change. Explain its effect on sound,
interaction, prediction, or numerical reliability. Check the crosswalk rather
than assuming matching scientific/engineering task suffixes identify the same work.

## Search and source inspection

| Query/site/date | Exact source ID and URL/DOI | Access achieved | Location inspected | Inclusion/exclusion reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| To complete | To complete | Identity/abstract/full text | Actual section/equation/table/page | Decision relevance |

- Prior ledger records reused and inspection dates:
- Unavailable full text/supplementary assets and retrieval outcome:
- Conflicting evidence and applicability:
- Precise requested asset/clarification; prepared request versus actually sent:

An abstract is not a methods review. Keep source status separate from claim
status. An adjacent-instrument method does not supply our specimen's parameters.

## Extraction and applicability

| Source claim/quantity | Original unit/notation | SI conversion and expression | Boundary/configuration | Origin and uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Narrow paraphrase and locator | Preserve definitions | Include sign/frame | Actual tested state | Measured/fitted/assumed/borrowed; interval meaning |

- What the source directly establishes:
- Project inference and reasoning:
- What cannot transfer to this instrument/model:
- Independent extraction check and discrepancy resolution:
- Rights/provenance scope for proposed assets/derivatives:

Unknown values remain null. For figure digitization record axes, method,
resolution, uncertainty, and only the minimum required data.

## Decision and model translation

- Outcome: accepted for stated scope / rejected / inconclusive / blocked:
- Claim status: candidate / supported_with_scope / contradicted / unresolved / withdrawn:
- Supported proposition and limits; interpretation that remains unsupported:
- Consequence: no change / parameter / equation / numerics / observation / measurement:
- Current file/symbol and proposed change; future paths explicitly labeled:
- Affected state, energy/work, ports, units, and failure behavior:
- Independent acceptance fixture and held-out prediction required:
- Promotion record needed, or reason no model promotion follows:

## Handoff

- Reviewer decision and unresolved disagreement:
- Evidence package and reproduction procedure:
- Downstream packages/reports potentially invalidated:
- Missing data/access/permission and permitted independent work:
- Smallest next action, owner, work order, and gate:

Retain rejected candidates and reasons. A successful optimizer, inspected paper,
or completed form alone does not pass a scientific phase.
